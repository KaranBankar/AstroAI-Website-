Kundli matching guide: what gun milan really means

A clear intro to kundli milan for marriage and relationships — without planet lectures.

When families search for kundli matching, they usually want one thing: will these two people feel steady together in daily life? Classical jyotish uses gun milan (ashtakoot) — up to 36 points — as a structured way to compare charts.

What ashtakoot looks at

The eight kootas cover themes like temperament, health rhythm, emotional pace, family harmony, and long-term staying power. A higher score can feel reassuring, but a mid score is common — and honesty about friction matters more than a perfect number.
